Official Match Ball · adidas · Unveiled 2 October 2025

Trionda

The official match ball of the FIFA World Cup 2026. Its name means “three waves” — a nod to the three host nations united for the first 48-team tournament.

★

Three nations, one ball

Made by adidas, Trionda carries the colours and icons of the co-hosts: red with a maple leaf for Canada, green with a golden eagle for Mexico, and blue with a five-pointed star for the United States. The three colours meet in a triangle at the centre of each panel — three nations coming together to host the World Cup for the first time — finished with gold detailing in homage to the FIFA World Cup Trophy.

Design & technology

Trionda is thermally bonded from just four panels — the fewest of any World Cup ball — in a shape inspired by the rolling “la ola” wave. The surface carries debossed macro and micro textures designed to improve flight stability, swerve and grip in wet conditions, so the ball flies true at the highest level.
