Official Mascots · Unveiled 25 September 2025

Meet the Mascots

For the first time, the FIFA World Cup has three official mascots — one for each host nation. Maple, Zayu and Clutch each play a position on the pitch and will star in the FIFA Heroes game.

01
The Moose · Goalkeeper · No. 1

Maple

Canada

An artistic, big-hearted moose who anchors the net wearing the No. 1. Maple represents creativity and resilience, and proudly carries the spirit of Canada.

09
The Jaguar · Forward · No. 9

Zayu

Mexico

A fearless jaguar and natural-born striker who wears the iconic green No. 9. Zayu plays up front with flair, energy and the passion of Mexican football.

★
The Bald Eagle · Midfielder

Clutch

United States

An adventurous bald eagle who runs the midfield with boundless curiosity and optimism, soaring across the host nation and embracing every game and moment.

A first for the World Cup

Maple, Zayu and Clutch were revealed on 25 September 2025 — the first time three mascots have represented a single FIFA World Cup, one for each of the co-hosts. Beyond the stadiums, the trio appear as playable characters in the FIFA Heroes game, each with a unique on-pitch ability alongside real World Cup stars.

About the imagery. The official mascot artwork is © FIFA, so it isn't reproduced here — each mascot is shown as a host-nation colour card. Names, animals, positions and details follow FIFA's official reveal.
